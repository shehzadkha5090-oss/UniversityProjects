Lab 13 — Causal Inference (OBSERVATIONAL)
Dataset: UCI Student Performance, student-por.csv (shared_dataset/dataset.csv),
  649 rows x 33 columns, semicolon-delimited. Outcome and treatment defined below.

CAVEAT — READ FIRST:
  There is NO randomised treatment in this dataset. This is an OBSERVATIONAL
  analysis and cannot prove causality. The result must NOT be read as
  "support causes passing". Any difference may reflect who was selected
  for support, not an effect of support itself.

Treatment and outcome
  Treatment: schoolsup (extra educational support).
  Group sizes: schoolsup = yes: 68 students; schoolsup = no: 581 students.
  Outcome: pass = (G3 >= 10). G3 was NEVER used as a feature (it defines the outcome).
  Raw pass rate, schoolsup = yes: 88.2% (60/68)
  Raw pass rate, schoolsup = no:  84.2% (489/581)
  Raw pass-rate difference (yes - no): +4.07 percentage points

Confounders used for the propensity score (stated list)
  school, sex, age, Medu, Fedu, studytime, failures, G1
  Justification: each plausibly affects BOTH receiving extra support and passing.
  G1 is a prior (first-period) grade, so it is pre-outcome and is a legitimate
  confounder here; G2/G3 were excluded because they are post-treatment / outcome.
  Categorical confounders (school, sex) were one-hot encoded; numeric confounders
  were standardised (StandardScaler). Model: LogisticRegression (max_iter=1000).
  Propensity-score range: 0.010 to 0.617;
    treated range 0.010 to 0.617,
    control range 0.010 to 0.555.
  Propensity scores were clipped to [0.01, 0.99] before IPW to avoid extreme weights.

Method 1 — IPW (inverse-probability weighting)
  IPW ATE on pass: +17.15 percentage points
  Standard error: bootstrap, 200 resamples, seed 42, full pipeline refit per resample: 14.39 percentage points
  Bootstrap 95% percentile interval: [-10.29, +40.64] percentage points
  (based on 200 successful bootstrap resamples)

Method 2 — Nearest-neighbour propensity matching
  Greedy matching on the propensity logit, caliper = 0.2 SD of the logit
  (logit SD = 1.087, caliper = 0.217 on the logit scale), without replacement.
  Treated units matched: 64 of 68 (94.1%)
  Matched pass rate, treated: 89.1%
  Matched pass rate, matched controls: 85.9%
  Matched ATE on pass: +3.12 percentage points
  Note: 4 treated unit(s) could not be matched within the caliper,
  so the matched ATE describes the matched subset only, not all treated students.

Covariate balance — standardised mean differences (SMD)
  |SMD| below ~0.1 is a common rough guide for acceptable balance.
  failures: before -0.002 | after IPW +0.140 | after matching +0.135
  G1: before -0.246 | after IPW +0.201 | after matching -0.147
  Medu: before -0.073 | after IPW -0.073 | after matching +0.000
  school (GP=1): before +0.440 | after IPW -0.273 | after matching -0.082
  Balance improved on all 4 checked variables after IPW weighting: False
  Balance improved on all 4 checked variables after matching: False

Interpretation — why this cannot be read causally
  Ignorability (no unmeasured confounding) is assumed by both IPW and matching:
  conditional on the stated confounders, receiving support must be as good as
  random. That assumption cannot be verified from this file.
  Unmeasured confounders plausibly remain, e.g. student motivation, teacher
  judgement about who needs help, prior difficulties not captured by G1/failures,
  and family circumstances beyond parental education. Support is typically GIVEN
  to students who are struggling, so selection bias can mask or mimic an effect.
  The raw gap, the IPW estimate and the matched estimate therefore describe
  adjusted ASSOCIATIONS under strong, unverifiable assumptions — not proof that
  support causes passing. Do not use these numbers to claim a causal effect.
